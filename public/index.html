    Rules

    Get all four of your tokens around the board and into the centre before anyone else.

    The die

    • 1, 2, 4 and 6 move one token that many squares.
    • Globus brings a token out of the base onto your start globe, or moves a token to the next globe. You roll again.
    • Star moves a token on the track to the next star.
    • Landing on a star with a normal roll carries the token on to the next star.

    Getting out

    • If all your remaining tokens are in the base, you get up to 3 rolls per turn to throw a globus.
    • Leaving the base onto your start globe sends any opponent standing there home.

    Capturing

    • Landing alone on one opponent token sends it back to its base.
    • Two or more tokens of one colour on a square defend it. Land there and your token goes home.
    • Globes protect. Land on a globe with an opponent on it and your token goes home.

    Finishing

    • You need the exact roll to reach the centre. Extra steps bounce back.
    • Only a globus gives another roll. Captures and reaching the centre do not.

    Connection

    • If you drop out, you have 60 seconds to rejoin. After that a bot plays your seat.
